Fun Facts

When I'm not thinking about AI safety, you might find me...

🚴

Cycling & Thinking

I do my best thinking while cycling. There's something about the rhythm of pedaling that unlocks new ideas. Some of my best research insights have come mid-ride.

🧮

Math for Calm

When stressed, I zone out with math problems. There's comfort in the certainty of proofs. It's my version of meditation.

🎵

Persian Music

I play the santur and daf, traditional Persian instruments that connect me to my heritage. Music is how I stay grounded.

💃

Dance Choreography

Creating and performing dance choreography is my creative outlet and way of expressing joy. Movement is another language.

🎨

Occasional Painting

I paint sometimes, though I wouldn't call myself a painter. It's about the process, not the product. Colors and textures help me see differently.

🌷

Simple Pleasures

Coffee, tea, tulips, and candles. The small things that make a space feel like home. I believe in creating beauty in everyday moments.

The Person Behind the Research

I believe that the best researchers bring their whole selves to their work. My artistic pursuits inform my research creativity. My cycling clears my mind for complex problems. My music connects me to a rich cultural heritage that shapes how I see the world.

AI safety isn't just a technical challenge - it's a deeply human one. Understanding people, cultures, and what makes life meaningful is essential to building AI that truly serves humanity.
