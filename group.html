Mentorship Program

Since 2023, I have run a one-year mentorship program for researchers and students worldwide, with limited spots each year. Mentees join a Discord channel, participate in reading group sessions, and have monthly one-on-ones covering graduate school applications, AI/ML research, math & programming foundations, job search, and career planning. Applications welcome in English or Farsi.

Undergraduate Research Internship

I am looking for motivated undergraduate research interns to join ongoing projects at the intersection of AI safety and large language models. Selected interns will work on cutting-edge research with the potential for publication at top-tier venues.

Research areas include:

  • AI Safety Foundations & Risk Mitigation
  • Safety Evaluation & Benchmarking
  • Reinforcement Learning & Alignment
  • Deceptive Alignment & Goal Misgeneralization
  • Factuality & Hallucination
  • Machine Unlearning & Memorization
  • Long-Context Learning
  • Test-Time Training
  • Emergent Behaviour

What I offer:

  • Hands-on research mentorship and regular one-on-ones
  • Guidance on research methodology, experimentation, and academic writing
  • Strong potential for co-authorship and publication at top AI venues
  • Career advice and connections across academia and industry

If you are an undergraduate student with a strong interest in any of the above areas and a willingness to commit to serious research, I encourage you to reach out with your CV and a brief note on why you are interested.
