Malikeh Ehghaghi

Hi, I am Malikeh (/mɒː.liːˈke/), a PhD student at the University of Toronto supervised by Colin Raffel. My research focuses on derisking and democratizing AI by building reliable and efficient machine learning systems that are robust under real-world uncertainty.

Research Interests

AI Safety & Robustness

Evaluating and improving the safety of large language models, including adversarial robustness and alignment.

LLM Capabilities & Evaluation

Creating better evaluation frameworks with a focus on probabilistic assessment.

Model Merging

Techniques to combine multiple models efficiently, enabling collaborative development.

Open-Source AI

Contributing to open-source AI development to ensure broad access to AI capabilities.

Democracy in AI

Working toward governance structures that give diverse stakeholders a voice.

Collaborative Learning

Distributed and federated learning methods that preserve privacy.
