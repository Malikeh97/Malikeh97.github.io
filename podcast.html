Women in AI Research (WiAIR) Podcast

A podcast spotlighting the voices of women shaping the future of AI research.

In 2025, I proudly co-hosted the Women in AI Research (WiAIR) podcast (Season 1) alongside Jekaterina Novikova, who played a formative role in my journey into AI research as my manager and industry supervisor during my MScAC, and someone I learned deeply from. In each episode, we spoke with accomplished women in AI from diverse backgrounds, exploring their research, personal journeys, and perspectives on real-world impact—aiming to inspire and empower the next generation of women in the field.
