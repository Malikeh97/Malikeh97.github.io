TokSuite: Measuring the Impact of Tokenizer Choice on Language Model Behavior

Gül Sena Altıntaş*, Malikeh Ehghaghi*, Brian Lester, Fengyuan Liu, Wanru Zhao, Marco Ciccone, Colin Raffel

TokSuite is a suite of 14 identical language models trained with different tokenizers, enabling controlled study of tokenization's isolated impact on model behavior. It introduces a new benchmark targeting real-world perturbations, revealing novel insights into the strengths and weaknesses of widely-used tokenizers.
