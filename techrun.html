TechRun Toronto

Building a vibrant Persian tech community in Toronto.

About TechRun

I founded TechRun to create a space for Persian tech professionals in Toronto to connect, share knowledge, and support each other. What started as a small meetup has grown into one of the largest Persian tech communities in Canada.

TechRun brings together engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and tech enthusiasts from the Persian diaspora. We organize regular meetups, workshops, and networking events to foster collaboration and mutual support.

What We Do

Meetups

Regular gatherings for networking, knowledge sharing, and community building.

Tech Talks

Presentations and discussions on the latest in technology, AI, and startups.

Mentorship

Connecting experienced professionals with newcomers to help navigate the Canadian tech scene.

Job Referrals

A supportive network for job seekers and companies looking for talented Persian tech professionals.

Join Us

Whether you're new to Toronto or a long-time resident, TechRun welcomes all Persian tech professionals. Join our community to connect with like-minded individuals and grow together.
