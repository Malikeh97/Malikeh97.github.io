Techrun Toronto

Building a vibrant Persian tech community in Toronto.

About Techrun

I founded Techrun to create a space for Persian tech professionals in Toronto to connect, share knowledge, and support each other. What started as a small meetup has grown into one of the largest Persian tech communities in Toronto.

Techrun brings together engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and tech enthusiasts from the Persian community. We organize regular meetups, workshops, and networking events to foster collaboration and mutual support.

What We Do

Meetups

Regular gatherings for networking, knowledge sharing, and community building.

Tech Chai ☕

A weekly virtual coffee chat every Friday at 5 PM EST, open to Persians worldwide. We discuss tech news, business and market updates, and host guest speakers — all over a warm cup of chai.
Co-hosted with Alireza Golkarieh.

Mentorship

Connecting experienced professionals with newcomers to help navigate the Canadian tech scene.

Job Referrals

A supportive network for job seekers and companies looking for talented Persian tech professionals.
